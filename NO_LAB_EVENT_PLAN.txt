BYTEFEST 2026 - NO LAB / TEAM DEVICE PLAN

Recommended setup
- One device per team for Code Sprint and Bug Hunt.
- A phone or tablet is enough; laptop is optional.
- Ask teams to keep the device in landscape mode where possible.
- Keep chargers/power banks and 2-3 spare devices with coordinators.
- Use the same Wi-Fi/hotspot area for all teams when possible.

Security behavior in v9
- The secure-mode gate appears only once in the participant session.
- On desktop/laptop, first start requests fullscreen.
- On phone/tablet, fullscreen is not required because mobile browser fullscreen support is inconsistent.
- Tab/app hiding still creates a security violation.
- Desktop focus-loss monitoring remains active.
- A new team login clears the previous device's secure-session flag.

Practical event rule
- One team leader controls the device; all team members may discuss.
- Do not permit calls, messaging apps, external search, AI tools, or switching apps during the challenge.
- Coordinator should keep 2-3 spare devices ready for genuine technical failures.
